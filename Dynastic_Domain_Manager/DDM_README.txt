DYNASTIC DOMAIN MANAGER
=======================

Version: 1.2
Supported CK3 branch: 1.19.*

Standalone realm-management interface for land distribution, succession, education and administration while keeping CK3's normal title and vassal rules.

MAIN FEATURES
-------------
- Domain Office: Overview, Crown Estate, Settlement, Dynasty, Realm Office and Ledger.
- Crown Estate protection and holding recommendations.
- Dynasty-first distribution with optional selected or eligible outsiders.
- Recipient scoring uses claims, de-jure fit, territory, capability, safety, family regard, succession and prior grants.
- Right-click title reservations for eligible recipients.
- Domain Limit and Vassal Limit management.
- Distribute to Proper Lieges hierarchy cleanup.
- Outsider holding protection and reclamation.
- War-aware queued settlement.
- Succession influence and dynastic alliance policy.
- Paged Dynasty Review with sortable character scores.
- Men-at-Arms advice, construction controls and administrative policies.
- Settlement Ledger and blocker diagnostics.

EDUCATION OFFICE
----------------
- Finds unattended educatable children among the ruler's children, court, House and Dynasty.
- Search range: Court, Realm, Dynasty or Diplomatic Range.
- Relevant guardian skill counts twice as much as Learning.
- Intelligence and temperament are smaller tie-breakers.
- Guardians at the ward limit are excluded.
- Assign All recalculates after each assignment.

AUTOMATIC EDUCATION WATCH
-------------------------
- Enabled by default and toggleable in the Education Office.
- Checks once per month through the quarterly playable pulse plus +1 and +2 month delayed checks.
- Searches for guardians only when an eligible child needs one.
- Also runs once after entering a save.

COMPATIBILITY
-------------
Standalone. No required gameplay-mod dependency.

INSTALLATION
------------
Place these in Documents/Paradox Interactive/Crusader Kings III/mod/:

  Dynastic Domain Manager.mod
  Dynastic_Domain_Manager/

Enable Dynastic Domain Manager in the Paradox Launcher.
