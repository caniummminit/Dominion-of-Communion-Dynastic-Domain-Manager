DYNASTIC DOMAIN MANAGER
=======================

Version: 1.0.0
Supported CK3 branch: 1.19.*

Dynastic Domain Manager is a standalone realm-management office for organizing
and distributing land while preserving the ruler's chosen personal estate. It
reduces repetitive title-granting, hierarchy cleanup, succession administration,
alliance upkeep and holding-management work without bypassing CK3's normal legal
rules for titles and vassals.

CORE FEATURES
-------------
DOMAIN OFFICE
- Unified management window for Crown Estate, Distribution, Recipients, Realm
  Hierarchy, Outsider Holdings, Succession, Alliances, Military, Construction
  and the settlement Ledger.
- Opening the office pauses the game. Workflows that genuinely require time to
  pass can resume processing and return to the office afterward.

CROWN ESTATE
- Explicitly choose which personally held titles are protected from settlement.
- The realm capital is always protected.
- Recommended holdings consider development, special buildings, military
  infrastructure and stationed Men-at-Arms.
- Registers can be organized by De Jure A-Z, Development, Faith or Culture and
  reversed without changing title ownership.

DISTRIBUTION
- Dynasty members are the default recipient pool.
- Recipient policy can remain Dynasty Only, prioritize Selected Candidates, or
  allow All Eligible Candidates.
- Eligible outsiders can include unlanded courtiers receiving their first grant
  as well as existing landed vassals.
- Claims, de-jure fit, existing territory, capability, political safety, family
  regard, succession relevance, current power and prior grants all influence
  placement.
- Selected candidates receive priority without forcing an arbitrary title on
  them; the allocator still looks for an appropriate legal grant.
- Higher titles are organized after county settlement to build a coherent realm.

TITLE RESERVATIONS
- Right-click an eligible candidate to reserve a specific county, duchy or
  kingdom for that character.
- Legal reservations are considered before ordinary scored allocation.
- Crown Estate, capital, rank, government, religion and normal grant legality
  still take precedence.
- Reservations can be cleared at any time.

REALM HIERARCHY
- Domain Limit and Vassal Limit are tracked separately.
- Distribute to Proper Lieges reorganizes eligible direct vassals beneath the
  nearest appropriate de-jure liege already inside the realm.
- This changes who reports directly to whom; it does not change title ownership.

OUTSIDER HOLDINGS
- Existing non-dynastic grants can be respected, selectively protected or
  reclaimed according to policy.
- Immediate reclamation returns eligible holdings to the Crown Estate without
  automatically redistributing them.
- Untransferable religious head-of-faith titles can be destroyed when necessary
  so underlying territorial holdings can be reclaimed cleanly.

WAR-AWARE SETTLEMENT
- The Domain Office remains available during war for planning and policy changes.
- A wartime Settle order can be queued until peace.
- When peace arrives, DDM settles if the plan remains valid or asks for Crown
  Estate confirmation if the situation changed materially.

SUCCESSION & ALLIANCES
- Succession can influence recipient ranking without becoming the only basis for
  distribution.
- Configurable alliance policy can maintain alliances with qualifying landed
  dynastic recipients.
- Post-succession cleanup can refresh the domain plan, title hierarchy, alliances
  and settlement records.

MILITARY & CONSTRUCTION
- Men-at-Arms adviser reviews the ruler's actual roster and CK3 unit families.
- Construction Office can issue bulk military-building programs across personally
  held provinces while preserving a configurable treasury reserve.
- Smart construction can prioritize the regiment stationed in each holding.

LEDGER
- Records the result of the most recent settlement so standing policies and
  completed actions remain easy to distinguish.

COMPATIBILITY
-------------
Dynastic Domain Manager is standalone. It has no required gameplay-mod dependency
and uses CK3's normal title and vassal legality.

INSTALLATION (MANUAL)
---------------------
Place both of the following directly in:
Documents/Paradox Interactive/Crusader Kings III/mod/

  Dynastic Domain Manager.mod
  Dynastic_Domain_Manager/

Enable Dynastic Domain Manager in the Paradox Launcher.
