DYNASTIC DOMAIN MANAGER — CHANGELOG
Compatibility target: Crusader Kings III 1.19.*

1.2 — CURRENT RELEASE
---------------------
DOMAIN OFFICE
- Consolidated routine management into Overview, Crown Estate, Settlement, Dynasty, Realm Office and Ledger.
- Added native Education, Dynasty Review, Military/Construction and Administrative Policy pages.
- Added paged Recipient, Outsider Holdings and Dynasty Review registers.
- Added clearer distribution score breakdowns.

EDUCATION
- Added Court, Realm, Dynasty and Diplomatic Range educator searches.
- Guardian scoring weights the child's relevant skill twice as strongly as Learning, with smaller intelligence and temperament adjustments.
- Excludes guardians at the two-ward limit.
- Added monthly automatic guardian assignment, enabled by default.
- Checks the ruler's children, court children, House children and Dynasty children.
- Skips adults, hostages, existing wards and Never Educate entries.
- Runs diplomatic-range guardian searches only when an eligible child needs one.
- Adds an immediate scan after entering a save.
