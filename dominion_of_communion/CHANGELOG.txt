DOMINION OF COMMUNION — CHANGELOG
Compatibility target: Crusader Kings III 1.19.*

1.2 — CURRENT RELEASE
-----------------------
FINAL COLLAPSE
- Added a middle outcome that lets a collapsed landed ruler keep current titles under Dominion.
- Bound Rulers surrender part of their wealth and pay increased tribute.
- Existing seizure and despair outcomes remain available.

LOVERS MANAGEMENT
- Added a paged Lovers & Soulmates register.
- Added individual and bulk Break Things Off actions.
- Pair-specific breakup exceptions prevent Dominion upkeep from recreating intentionally ended relationships.

LEDGER & PERFORMANCE
- Added 20-row paging to long roster pages while keeping full row details and sorting.
- World Cucks keeps the full lightweight roster and builds detailed rows only for the current page.
- Removed the old 500-row presentation limit.
- Added CK3 skill icons to roster stat displays.
- Removed obsolete explanatory footer text.

OWNED & TRIBUTE
- Added tiered passive Prestige, Piety, Renown and quarterly Legitimacy from living Owned subjects.
- Clarified automatic tribute/intelligence and manual yearly collection.

OBEDIENCE & JEALOUSY
- Added configurable Obedience stage consequences.
- Added partner reactions when the Patron takes a new partner.
- Attached-or-higher Obedience protects established relationships from jealousy breakups.

ALPHA & TANTALIZE
- Spousal humiliation now affects related Alpha/Tantalize pressure.
- Alpha Gauntlet now resolves the full roster and scores the overall result.

REPAIRS
- Fixed Enforce Dominion gating and paused-game lockout.
- Improved Endowment trait expression and save cleanup.
- Reworked Cucked stigma storage to avoid large permanent per-character opinion records.
- Cucked status uses a vanilla trait icon instead of a missing-texture question mark.

CARNALITAS
- Dominion measurements remain authoritative with the compatibility module loaded.
- Carnalitas inheritance data remains synchronized without duplicate visible size traits.

1.0.1 — HOTFIX
----------------
- Made Endowment tiers mutually exclusive in Ruler Designer.
- Prevented invalid multi-tier custom rulers and related startup crashes.

1.0.0 — FIRST PUBLIC RELEASE
----------------------------
- Added the Communion Ledger.
- Added Alpha dominance, Cuck Experience, Tantalize, Obedience and Pet progression.
- Added Owned subjects, Household Dominion, tribute and intelligence.
- Added Household Intrigue, blood acknowledgement and succession pressure.
- Added persistent Endowment measurements and individual preferences.
- Added World Cuck tracking, Crowned Union and AI participation.
- Added optional Carnalitas compatibility.
